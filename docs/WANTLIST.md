# THE WANTLIST
### Data the survey hungers for

The organizing question, verbatim from the maintainer: **what is the data that would
really tell us about the soul of the world?** Not what is downloadable. Not what is
convenient. What would a future historian need to reconstruct how it felt to be us?

**The law of this document:** entries are organized by *soul question*. Feasibility is
an attribute of an entry, never the reason it exists or dies.

**Status legend (deliberately unverified):** this round is the brainstorm; verification
is the acquisition pass. `OPEN?` = believed open · `GATED?` = believed paywalled or
ToS-restricted, gate named · `DARK` = no dataset exists; we build it ourselves from
primary records, cited entry by entry. A ✓ appears only after the verification pass
confirms access, granularity, and license.

**Forms:** `points` (with stories) · `heat` (density/tint — groups, never individuals) ·
`flows` (arcs — new render form) · `raster` (continuous field — new render form).

---

## THE PROTOCOL (binding)

1. **Suicide and self-harm data ships as heat only.** Rates and aggregates; never
   incident points, never methods, never ages below published thresholds. Federal
   suppression rules are honored absolutely — small counts stay dark, disclosed as
   dark. The plate carries crisis resources (988) above the essay. Safe-messaging
   practice governs all copy: no sensationalizing, no simplistic causes.
2. **Fraud and corruption entries name individuals only from conviction records and
   major-outlet reporting.** Every ledger entry carries its citations. Allegations
   without convictions are not data.
3. **No doxxing, ever.** Nothing on this map should locate a private living person.
4. **The dead are handled like the living.** Unclaimed-persons data ships as counts
   and stories already public, never as searchable identities.
5. Everything else in the survey's standing law applies: no fabricated records,
   provenance sidecars, gates named honestly, PENDING slots instead of fakes.

## THE DATA THAT DOES NOT EXIST

An honest survey names its missing instruments. There is no dataset of roadside
memorials — the crosses on the shoulders of the roads our Loss layer already draws.
No registry of church closures. No map of estrangement, of unsent letters, of the
distance between where people live and where they wish they lived. The survey's
PENDING slots are promises; this list is the horizon. If a reader knows where one of
these datasets hides, the layer-proposal template is open.

## THE VERIFICATION PASS (next round, per selected candidate)

For each of the ~20 selected: confirm exact URL and endpoint · granularity (county /
tract / point) · vintage and update rhythm · suppression rules · license and
redistribution terms · record count and size · the two-line method a surveyor would
run. Only then do acquisitions begin. Selection precedes verification precedes
download — in that order, deliberately.
